# vFM SD card

**SD destination:** `/vFM/`

Copy the CONTENTS of the repository sdcard folder into /vFM on the microSD. The player creates folders and these guides; banks and presets must be copied from a PC. Keep ordinary music outside /vFM. Keep MBK, MWK, MUP, PDX, PSFLIB and GSFLIB companions beside their songs. Merge updates without replacing your settings or recordings.

[Files and instructions](https://github.com/PanykSystem/vFMPlayer/tree/main/sdcard)

Download the repository ZIP and copy the contents of `sdcard` into `/vFM` on the microSD. Keep existing personal settings and recordings. The adjacent `read.me` contains the same essentials in plain text.
